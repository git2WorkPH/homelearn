# User personas

Status: Draft. All examples are synthetic.

| Persona | Needs | Design implications |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner parent | Understand goals, methods, planning and evidence | Resumable guidance, explanations, original starter templates |
| Experienced parent | Control curricula, schedules and assessment | Direct editing, custom definitions, optional guidance |
| Multi-child parent | Different preferences and individual progress | Shared activities with separate learner assignments and observations |

Adults are the authenticated users. Learners do not require accounts. Minimise learner information; exact birth dates and sensitive diagnoses are not prerequisites for the proposed workflow.

Tutors, groups, publishers and community organisers are future personas, excluded from MVP implementation.
