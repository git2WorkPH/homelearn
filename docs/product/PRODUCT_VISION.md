# Product vision

Status: Draft specification. Documentation creation approved on 19 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney); implementation and unresolved design decisions are not approved by that permission.

HomeLearn is a parent-led, child-centred homeschooling platform. It adapts to the family through a continuous workflow: choose goals, plan learning, record experiences, attach evidence, review progress and export summaries.

Guided Mode offers explanations and optional suggestions. Independent Mode offers direct control. Both use the same records, and switching modes preserves all educational data. Parent decisions are authoritative. Core functionality works without AI.

Initial synthetic scenarios use learners aged 5 and 8 in NSW; these are not age restrictions. Design family ownership from the beginning while deferring commercial, community and tutor capabilities.

Curriculum, pedagogy, scheduling and assessment are separate concepts. Activity completion does not demonstrate mastery. Official references retain provenance; the product never certifies regulatory compliance.
